## Hi, I'm Trong-Tin (Tommy) Nguyen

I'm a dedicated researcher and lecturer in Economics and Econometrics. I specialize in Convex Optimization and data manipulation using Stata, R, and Python. My proficiency in these tools helps me analyze data effectively, supporting well-informed decision-making and providing crucial insights for strategic choices.

I'm strongly devoted to sharing knowledge and guidance with the next generation in Economics and Econometrics. I believe that the combination of research and teaching is the best way to contribute to this field's growth.

Please don't hesitate to contact me if you're interested in collaborating or discussing potential projects!

### Research

- **Growth and institutions:** intertemporal general equilibrium models of growth, innovation, taxation and corruption
- **Climate and trade policy:** applied CGE modelling (GTAP) of carbon pricing and the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism
- **Public finance and development:** applied econometrics on foreign aid, governance and public expenditure
- **Sustainability:** circular economy and agroforestry for rural livelihoods and land restoration in Vietnam
